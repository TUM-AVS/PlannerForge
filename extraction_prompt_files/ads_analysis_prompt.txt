[CP]
Failure-mode → suggested-change rules:

1. **High collision rate (collision % > 20%)**
   - RAISE `distance_to_obstacles` (e.g., +0.3 to +0.5 absolute).
   - Optionally raise `prediction` and `responsibility` (+0.1 to +0.2)
     so the planner trusts other-actor forecasts more.
   - Do NOT touch jerk / acceleration weights here — they aren't the
     cause of collisions.

2. **High max_steps rate (timeouts > 20%)**
   - RAISE `velocity_offset` (+0.3 to +0.5) so the planner penalises
     low-speed trajectories more aggressively.
   - LOWER `lateral_jerk` and `longitudinal_jerk` (−0.1 each) so the
     planner is allowed to accelerate harder.
   - Do NOT raise `distance_to_obstacles` here — that would make the
     ego more cautious and worsen timeouts.

3. **Frequent "no valid or feasible trajectory found"
   (infeasibility > 10%)**
   - LOWER `distance_to_reference_path` (e.g., 5.0 → 2.0) so the
     planner can deviate further from the reference.
   - RAISE `path_length` (+0.2 to +0.5) to allow longer alternative
     paths.

4. **Mixed failures** (no single mode dominates)
   - Snap to the `Balanced` preset (see below) — well-rounded weights
     that are a reasonable starting point when no single mode wins.

General constraints:
- Keep every individual change ≤ 0.5 in absolute delta unless the
  failure rate exceeds 50%.
- Values stay non-negative.
- Do NOT add, remove, or reorder parameters. The 13 + 3 names must be
  identical between input and output.

5. **When incremental tuning is risky** (e.g., the current cost.yaml is
   far from any named preset, or several failure modes co-occur near
   their thresholds), you may **snap to one of the named presets below
   instead** — copy its 13 cost_weights values verbatim and leave
   external_cost_weights untouched. Pick the preset whose intent
   matches the desired post-tune behavior:

   - `Default` — minimal, mostly zeros. Good when you need to reset
     after an aggressive tune went wrong.
   - `Comfort` — strong lateral/longitudinal jerk damping. Pick when
     the batch shows oscillation, harsh manoeuvres, or passenger
     discomfort proxies.
   - `Balanced` — well-rounded; the current production-tuned default.
     Pick when the batch is in the middle ground (no dominant mode).
   - `Efficiency-Sporty` — high velocity weight, lower jerk damping.
     Pick when max_steps timeouts dominate AND the road geometry is
     simple (highway/straight).
   - `Safety-Conservative` — high distance_to_obstacles + prediction
     + responsibility. Pick when collisions dominate (>30%) and you
     want a bigger fix than the +0.4 incremental bump in Rule 1.
